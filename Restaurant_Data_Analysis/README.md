# A Data Analysis Project

## Project Details
 
- **Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook  
- **Dataset:** Restaurant Dataset (CSV)

---

## 1. Project Overview

**

The objective of this project is to analyse a restaurant dataset to identify trends related to cuisines, cities, pricing, ratings, votes, and service availability, and to present these findings in a clear and professional manner.

---

## 2. Dataset Description

The dataset contains information about restaurants from various cities, including customer ratings, pricing categories, cuisine types, and service features.

### Key Columns:
- **Restaurant ID** – Unique identifier for each restaurant
- **Restaurant Name** – Name of the restaurant
- **Country Code** - Numeric country identifier
- **City** – City where the restaurant is located
- **Address / Locality / Locality Verbose** - Location details
- **Longitude & Latitude** - Geographic coordinates
- **Cuisines** - One or more cuisines served
- **Average Cost for Two** – Typical cost for two people
- **Currency** – Currency used
- **Price Range** – Budget to premium classification
- **Aggregate Rating** – Overall customer rating
- **Rating Text / Rating Colour** – Rating category information
- **Votes** – Number of customer votes  
- **Has Table Booking** – Indicates Availability of table booking  
- **Has Online Delivery** – Indicates online delivery availability  
- **Has Table Booking** – Indicates table booking availability
    

This dataset enables analysis of customer preferences, service quality, and pricing strategies.

---

## 3. Tasks Performed

### &star; Level 1 Tasks
- **Top Cuisines Analysis** – Identified the most common cuisines and their percentage distribution.
- **City-wise Restaurant Analysis** – Determined cities with the highest number of restaurants and highest average ratings.
- **Price Range Distribution** – Visualised how restaurants are distributed across price ranges.
- **Online Delivery Analysis** – Analysed the impact of online delivery on restaurant ratings.

### &star; Level 2 Tasks
- **Cuisine Combination Analysis** – Identified popular cuisine combinations and evaluated their ratings.
- **Geographic Analysis** – Analysed restaurant distribution patterns based on available location data.
- **Restaurant Chains Analysis** – Identified restaurant chains and analysed their popularity.

### &star; Level 3 Tasks
- **Votes Analysis** – Identified restaurants with the highest and lowest votes.
- **Rating vs Votes Correlation** – Studied the relationship between customer engagement and ratings.
- **Price Range vs Services** – Analysed the relationship between price range and service availability.

## 6. Key Insights

- Certain cuisines dominate the restaurant market.
- Major cities have a higher concentration of restaurants.
- Restaurants offering online delivery tend to receive higher ratings.
- There is a positive correlation between votes and ratings.
- Higher-priced restaurants often provide additional services.
